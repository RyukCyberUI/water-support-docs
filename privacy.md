# Water Support Bot — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Water Support Bot respects user privacy and is designed to process only the limited information necessary to provide Discord-based support ticket services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the application processes, how that information is used, and how users may request its deletion.

## 2. Information Collected

Water Support Bot operates primarily as a ticket creation and management application.

The information processed is limited to the following categories:

- **Ticket Content:** Messages, descriptions, attachments, and other information voluntarily submitted by users within support tickets.
- **Discord Identifiers:** Basic identifiers, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, and ticket channel IDs, when technically necessary to create and manage tickets.
- **Ticket Information:** Basic details associated with ticket creation, status, and closure.

The application is not intended to collect unrelated private conversations, passwords, payment credentials, browsing history, or personal information unrelated to support requests.

## 3. Purpose of Data Processing

Information is processed exclusively for legitimate support-related activities, including:

- Creating and organizing support tickets.
- Identifying the user requesting assistance.
- Allowing authorized staff members to respond to inquiries.
- Investigating and resolving reported technical issues.
- Maintaining the proper operation of the ticket system.

User information is not collected for advertising, marketing, or unrelated commercial profiling.

## 4. Data Access and Confidentiality

Ticket information is intended to be accessible only to the relevant user and authorized Water Support personnel, subject to the permissions configured in Discord.

Support representatives are expected to handle submitted information responsibly and maintain appropriate confidentiality.

Information is not sold to advertisers or data brokers.

Discord and any infrastructure providers involved in operating the application may process information according to their applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data Storage and Retention

Messages submitted within support tickets are stored through Discord's platform.

Ticket information is retained only as reasonably necessary for support management, subject to applicable retention requirements and the actual configuration of the ticket system.

Closing a support ticket does not necessarily mean that all associated messages or records have been permanently deleted.

## 6. Data Security

Water Support aims to protect user information through appropriate access controls and responsible administrative practices.

Access to support tickets should be restricted to authorized personnel.

Although reasonable precautions are taken, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request information about their support-related data or request deletion of eligible information.

Such requests can be submitted by contacting authorized Water Support personnel through the official Discord support server.

Requests will be reviewed and handled in accordance with applicable privacy requirements and technical limitations.

## 8. Third-Party Services

Water Support Bot operates through Discord's infrastructure and is subject to relevant Discord platform policies.

For additional information regarding Discord's handling of personal information, please review:

https://discord.com/privacy

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Water Support may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application functionality, operational requirements, or applicable regulations.

Updates will be published on this page.

## 10. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries, data-related concerns, or deletion requests, please contact the Water Support team through the official Discord support server.
